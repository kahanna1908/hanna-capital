A market-neutral long/short fund across the 11 U.S. sector ETFs. It goes long the strongest sectors, short the weakest, and hedges out the stock market with SPY. A script runs after every market close, makes the trades, and updates this page. Every trade is time-stamped in the public code history, so the track record can't be edited after the fact.
